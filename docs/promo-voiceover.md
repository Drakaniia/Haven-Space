# Haven Space promo — voiceover

Make one ElevenLabs clip per block and place each clip at the start of its scene, which makes syncing easiest. For a single take, paste the blocks in order instead. Total ~60s.

Read: warm, upbeat, young-adult, conversational. A friend tipping you off, not a radio announcer.

## 1. Hook (~6s)

```text
Still asking around for a boarding house near your school?
Got a room that's been empty for weeks?
```

## 2. Meet the app (~5s)

```text
There's an easier way.
Meet Haven Space.
One app. Find a place. Fill your rooms.
```

## 3. Students (~14s)

```text
Students — search by school, budget, or right on the map.
Compare prices, browse photos, and see what's still available.
Like one? Request a tour or message the landlord, right from the listing.
Affordable. Nearby. Easy to find.
```

## 4. Landlords (~13s)

```text
Landlords — have empty rooms? Put them where students are looking.
Add your photos, pin your location, and set your prices and room details.
Get verified, go live, and manage every listing from one place.
```

## 5. The connection (~9s)

```text
A landlord lists a room.
A student nearby finds it, checks the photos... and requests a tour.
Just like that — connected.
```

## 6. Benefits (~6s)

```text
For students: more choices, less searching.
For landlords: more visibility, fewer empty rooms.
```

## 7. Call to action (~10s)

```text
Looking for a place? Start searching.
Have a room to rent? List it today.
Haven Space. Find your next place. Fill your next room.
```
